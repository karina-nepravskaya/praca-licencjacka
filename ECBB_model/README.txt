This directory contains results from the simulations that were used for the paper:

A. Emmanouilidou, M. B. Peters, and G. P. Katsoulis, "Singularity in the electron-core potential as a gateway to accurate multielectron ionization spectra in strongly driven atoms", Phys. Rev. A 107, L041101 (2023).


We study nonsequential triple ionization in Ne interacting with a laser field along the z axis. The pulse duration in intensity is 25 fs while the wavelength is 800 nm. We consider the intensities 1, 1.3 and 1.6 PW/cm^2.
The txt files in this directory contain the final momenta of all the particles for all the events, and for the events of the two main pathways, namely, the direct (e,3e) and the delayed (e,2e) pathways.
We note that:

i) In the direct (e,3e) pathway, all three electrons ionize soon after recollision.

ii) In the delayed (e, 2e) pathway, the recolliding electron transfers enough energy for only two electrons to ionize soon after recollision, while the other electron ionizes with a delay.

The percentage contribution of the two main pathways is:

Intensity     direct (e,3e) pathway        delayed (e,2e) pathway

1.0 PW/cm^2 	     41.9 %			 43.0 %

1.3 PW/cm^2 	     45.0 %			 42.4 %

1.6 PW/cm^2 	     47.4 %			 40.7 %



We label the particles as:

particle 1 = core
particle 2 = initially bound electron
particle 3 = initially bound electron
particle 4 = initially tunnelling electron

In the given files, we print in the following order

p1x p1y p1z p2x p2y p2z p3x p3y p3z p4x p4y p4z

where pix is the x-component of the final momentum of particle i. Similarly, for piy and piz.
